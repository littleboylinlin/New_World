## v0.26.0 更新内容

- **开场动画（桌面版）**　冷启动时先像打字一样写出「New World」，名字淡出后，那扇门移到中央、关上，再推开进入主页，主页随之入场。全程约 2.5 秒，与应用加载同时进行；点击或按任意键可跳过，按下的键照常生效。只在打开应用时播放，开新窗口、阅读窗口或从托盘唤回都不播放；窗口在后台时会直接结束。系统开启「减少动态效果」时不播放，也可以在「设置 › 外观」关闭。
- **主页提醒与待办**　主页新增「提醒与待办」，按已逾期、今天、接下来 7 天和有提醒的顺序列出最近的待办，可以直接勾选完成。点击关联了作品的待办会打开那部作品，有章节和位置的直接跳到那里；其他待办打开详情。宽屏时它在右侧一栏，窄屏时排在写作统计下面。主页内容改为从顶部开始排列。
- **大窗口与全屏**　窗口较宽时，主页、书库、灵感和待办的内容区会随之放宽，书架、最近项目和灵感列表一行能放下更多，单张卡片不会被放大；正文栏宽仍由外观设置决定。设置页在宽屏下分两列。很宽的屏幕上，编辑器的章节目录和右侧面板会稍微放宽。桌面版支持全屏：macOS 全屏时标题栏不再给红绿灯留空位；Windows 按 F11 进入全屏后，最小化和最大化换成「退出全屏」。
- **平滑光标开关**　「设置 › 外观」新增「平滑光标与打字」，默认开启。关闭后正文和所有输入框都使用系统原生光标。
- **最近项目显示方式**　最近项目可以在列表和封面两种样式之间切换，选择会保存在本机。
- **菜单不再挤动页面**　下拉菜单在下方放不下时会向上展开，上下都放不下时在菜单内部滚动，不再把页面往上推。
- **回收站**　侧栏的回收站不再显示红点。

### 兼容性

书库数据库仍为 v10，工程仍使用 Schema 3，全库备份仍使用格式 2。新增的界面设置只保存在本机。

## What's New in v0.26.0

- **Opening animation (desktop)**　On a cold start “New World” types itself out, the name fades, and the door moves to the middle, shuts, and swings open onto the home page, which comes in behind it. It takes about 2.5 seconds while the app loads; a click or any key skips it, and the key still does what it does. It plays only when the app opens, never for new windows, reading windows, or when the app comes back from the tray, and it ends at once if the window is in the background. It never plays with reduced motion, and it can be turned off in Settings › Appearance.
- **Reminders and tasks on the home page**　The home page now lists upcoming tasks: overdue, today, the next 7 days, and those with reminders. Tick them off right there. A task linked to a work opens that work, at the chapter and spot it points to; other tasks open their details. On wide screens the list sits in its own column on the right; on narrow ones it comes after the writing stats. The home page now starts at the top.
- **Large windows and full screen**　In wide windows the home, library, ideas, and tasks pages use more of the width: shelves, recent projects, and idea lists fit more per row without enlarging each card, and the text column still follows your appearance settings. Settings shows two columns on wide screens, and on very wide screens the editor's chapter list and side panel get a little wider. The desktop app supports full screen: on macOS the header no longer leaves room for the window buttons, and on Windows, after F11, minimize and maximize give way to “Exit full screen”.
- **Smooth caret switch**　Settings › Appearance has a new “Smooth caret and typing” switch, on by default. Turn it off to use the system caret in the text and in every field.
- **Recent projects layout**　Recent projects can be shown as a list or as covers, and the choice is saved on this device.
- **Menus stay in place**　A menu that does not fit below opens upward, and one that fits neither way scrolls inside itself instead of pushing the page up.
- **Trash**　The trash in the sidebar no longer shows a red dot.

### Compatibility

The library database remains at v10, projects still use Schema 3, and full-library backups still use format 2. The new interface settings are stored only on this device.
