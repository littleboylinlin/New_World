## 简体中文与英语

- 新增英语界面，覆盖书库、阅读、编辑、导出、回收站、设置、错误提示和桌面菜单。
- 安装器根据系统语言只下载一种语言包：中文系统使用简体中文，其他系统使用英语。语言包经过大小和 SHA-256 校验，下载失败可以重试。
- 在「设置 → 语言」切换中英文；需要时再下载对应语言包。已下载的语言可以离线使用，升级后保留选择。
- 切换语言前先保存正文。作品名、章节名和正文保持原样。

v0.7.0 及以上版本可通过应用内「帮助与更新」升级。首次安装或首次切换到尚未下载的语言需要联网，已有书库可继续使用。

## Simplified Chinese and English

- English is available throughout the library, reader, editor, export tools, trash, settings, error messages, and desktop menus.
- The installer downloads only the language matching your system: Simplified Chinese for Chinese systems, or English otherwise. Packs are checked for size and SHA-256 integrity.
- Switch languages in Settings → Language. Additional packs download only when needed; downloaded languages work offline and your choice is preserved across upgrades.
- Your text is saved before switching. Work titles, chapter titles, and manuscript text stay unchanged.

Versions 0.7.0 and later can upgrade through Help and updates. A connection is needed for the first installation or the first use of an additional language. Existing libraries remain available.
