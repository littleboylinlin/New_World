## v0.22.0 更新内容

- **侧栏导航**　主页、写作、灵感与任务、归档、回收站排在左侧，设置固定在底部。侧栏可以收起成图标栏，收起状态会被记住。进入作品时侧栏暂时收起，离开后恢复。窄屏下侧栏改为从菜单按钮打开的抽屉，可用遮罩、Esc 或键盘关闭。
- **主页**　保留写作热力图和统计数字，最近项目按打开顺序列出两类作品，最多八部。升级前的记录保持原有顺序，重新打开一次后显示真实时间。书库为空时可直接新建作品。
- **写作**　原创和续写·仿写合并为一个书架，可按全部、原创、续写·仿写筛选，卡片标注类型。支持按书名搜索，可按最近加入、最近打开或书名排序，筛选和排序会被记住。新建作品时先选择类型。导入 `.newworld` 工程时自动识别类型，普通原稿在选择类型后导入。
- **灵感与任务**　两者合并为一个入口，顶部切换分区并记住选择。灵感可以按正文搜索，可以转为任务，转换后直接进入该任务的详情。
- **桌面速记**　全局快捷键唤起的小窗默认记录灵感，按 Tab 可切换到提醒。只选时间时，提醒定在下一次到达该时间的时刻，旁边会预览具体日期。只选日期时保存为计划日期，两项都不填时保存为收件箱任务。
- **后台自启动**　桌面版可以在设置中开启登录后在后台启动，默认关闭。后台启动时托盘、快捷键和提醒照常工作。卸载 Windows 版时会清理启动项。网页版不显示此设置。
- **旧链接兼容**　原来两个书架、灵感和任务的链接会跳转到对应的筛选或分区，作品详情链接保持不变。

### 兼容性

书库数据库仍为 v10，工程仍使用 Schema 3，全库备份仍使用格式 2。新的筛选、排序、分区和侧栏状态只保存在本机。

## What's New in v0.22.0

- **Sidebar navigation**　Home, Writing, Ideas & tasks, Archive, and Trash are listed on the left, with Settings pinned to the bottom. You can collapse the sidebar to icons, and the app remembers your choice. The sidebar collapses while you work on a piece and returns when you leave. On narrow screens, the sidebar becomes a drawer opened from the menu button and closed with the backdrop, Esc, or the keyboard.
- **Home**　The writing heatmap and statistics remain. Recent projects list both kinds of works in the order you opened them, up to eight. Entries from earlier versions keep their order and show a real time after you open them again. When the library is empty, you can create a work directly from Home.
- **Writing**　Originals and continuations now share one shelf. Filter by All, Original, or Continuation / imitation, and each card shows its type. Search by title and sort by date added, last opened, or title. The app remembers your filter and sort order. New works start by choosing a type. Importing a `.newworld` project detects its type automatically, and plain manuscripts are imported after you choose a type.
- **Ideas & tasks**　Ideas and tasks now share one entry with a switch at the top, and the app remembers the section you used last. You can search ideas by text and turn an idea into a task, which then opens in the task details.
- **Quick capture**　The window opened by the global shortcut records an idea by default. Press Tab to switch to a reminder. If you set only a time, the reminder uses the next occurrence of that time and previews the date. If you set only a date, it is saved as a planned date. If you leave both empty, it is saved to the Inbox.
- **Start in the background**　On desktop, you can choose in Settings to start in the background at login. This option is off by default. Background starts keep the tray, shortcut, and reminders working. Uninstalling on Windows removes the startup entry. The web version does not show this setting.
- **Older links**　Links to the previous shelves, ideas, and tasks redirect to the matching filter or section. Links to individual works are unchanged.

### Compatibility

The library database remains at v10, projects still use Schema 3, and full-library backups still use format 2. The new filter, sort, section, and sidebar settings are stored only on this device.
