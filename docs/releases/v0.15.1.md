## AI 数据保护、回收站隔离与自动历史版本恢复加固

- **回收站独立最终防线与严格隔离**：严禁 AI 通过官方 `/v1` HTTP 接口读取、恢复、清空或修改回收站、章节历史版本或备份数据（统一返回 `403 recovery_data_forbidden`）；灵感删除现在也会在单事务中完整迁入回收站（`ideas` 恢复支持）；确认删除后的作品、原文附件、版本、章节与灵感均在可恢复存储中保留，仅允许用户在 New_World 界面手动恢复或经独立二次确认后彻底删除。
- **AI 改稿前自动保留历史版本（原子事务）**：在 IndexedDB 新增持久化 `revisions` 与 `aiBaselines` 存储；每次 AI 成功更新单章（`chapters.update`）或批量替换章节（`chapters.replaceBatch`）前，均在同一严格事务中先保存修改前完整正文与元数据，若快照保存失败则原子中止更新、绝不覆盖原稿；连续修改独立保留每次历史且不可通过 `skipHistory` / `preserveHistory` 参数关闭。编辑器内新增「历史版本」查看、预览与无损恢复弹窗，恢复旧版前也会先自动备份当前正文。
- **修复防破坏检查缺口与审计溯源**：拦截 1–4 字短章被 AI 清空，引入 token-bigram 语义留存度识别「等字数全文替换」与跨程序重启持续有效的累计改稿基线，修正等字数替换确认窗中的 `(-0)` 字数提示，并在本地审计日志中记录快照与回收站凭据。

提供 Windows x64、Mac Apple Silicon 和 Intel 安装包。SHA-256 校验值见 `SHA256SUMS.txt`。

## AI data protection, trash isolation & pre-edit revision recovery

- **Strict isolation of Trash, revision history & backups**: All `/v1/trash*`, `/v1/revisions*`, `/v1/history*`, and `/v1/backups*` routes are unconditionally denied (`403 recovery_data_forbidden`), and `skipHistory: true` / `preserveHistory: false` flags are rejected (`403 history_protection_non_bypassable`). Deleted works, continuation sources, editions, chapters, and ideas (`kind: 'idea'`) move atomically into recoverable Trash storage and can only be restored or permanently deleted (with an independent confirmation dialog) from the New_World UI.
- **Atomic pre-edit revision snapshots & UI recovery**: Upgrades IndexedDB to schema v6 (`revisions`, `aiBaselines`). Every `chapters.update` and `chapters.replaceBatch` atomically writes complete pre-edit snapshots in a single strict transaction before modifying content—aborting the mutation cleanly if snapshot persistence fails. Consecutive AI edits preserve distinct history entries, and a new in-editor History dialog previews revisions and automatically backs up current text before restoring an older version.
- **Closed destructive-update guard gaps**: Prevents clearing short (`<5`-word) chapters, detects equal-word wholesale replacement and cumulative rewrites across restarts via persisted baselines, fixes misleading `(-0)` word-change text, and records snapshot/trash receipts in audit logs.

Includes Windows x64 and separate Mac Apple Silicon and Intel installers. Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
