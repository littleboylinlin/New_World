## v0.25.0 更新内容

- **新图标**　图标换成只用线条的「推开的一页」，分浅色和深色两套。网页版的标签页图标跟随系统明暗，侧栏图标跟随应用外观；桌面安装包也换上了新图标。
- **灵感与待办**　编辑器右侧的「灵感」标签改为「灵感与待办」，用分段按钮在两者之间切换。待办与待办页使用同一套列表和弹窗，可以只看本作品，也可以看全部，正在写的这一章的待办排在最前。从章节或选中文字新建待办后，会自动切到待办。
- **对照整个书库**　对照列表按「作品 → 版本 → 章节」分层列出整个书库，原文文件跟在作品下面。本作品排在最前并默认展开，也列出它的其他版本，正在编辑的一章标「编辑中」。作品、版本、章节都可以拖进编辑区或右键选择打开位置，展开状态会记住；搜索能搜到作品、版本和章节名，命中时自动展开。
- **提示不挡操作**　普通提示在点击任意位置或按下任意键时消失，这次点击或按键照常生效。提示不再让桌面版的网页块暂时无法操作，而是自动避开网页块；在网页里点击也会关闭提示。带「撤销」的提示会保留一段时间，鼠标停在上面时暂停计时。
- **长文件转换进度**　把较长的 TXT 等原文件转换为编辑稿时，会依次显示转换、保存章节和打开的进度；打开很长的章节前也会先提示正在排版。

### 兼容性

书库数据库仍为 v10，工程仍使用 Schema 3，全库备份仍使用格式 2。

## What's New in v0.25.0

- **New icon**　The icon is now a line drawing of a page swinging open, in a light and a dark version. On the web the tab icon follows your system's light or dark mode, and the sidebar icon follows the app's appearance; the desktop installers use the new icon too.
- **Ideas and to-dos**　The “Notes” tab beside the editor is now “Ideas & to-dos”, with a segmented switch between the two. To-dos use the same list and dialogs as the to-do page and can show this work only or everything, with the chapter you are writing first. A to-do made from a chapter or from selected text switches to the to-dos side.
- **Compare across the library**　The Compare list shows the whole library by work, version, and chapter, with original files under their work. The current work comes first and starts expanded, now including its other versions, and the chapter being edited is marked. Works, versions, and chapters can all be dragged into the editor or opened from the context menu. Expanded groups are remembered, and search finds works, versions, and chapters and expands to them.
- **Notices stay out of the way**　A plain notice goes away with your next click or key press anywhere, and that click or key still does what it does. Notices no longer make a desktop web pane unusable; they move clear of it instead, and clicking inside the page also closes them. A notice with Undo stays for a while and pauses while the pointer rests on it.
- **Progress for long conversions**　Turning a long TXT or other original file into a manuscript now shows its progress through converting, saving chapters, and opening. Opening a very long chapter says so before the layout starts.

### Compatibility

The library database remains at v10, projects still use Schema 3, and full-library backups still use format 2.
