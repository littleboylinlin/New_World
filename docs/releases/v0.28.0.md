## v0.28.0 更新内容

这一版专注把界面做轻：去掉层层套叠的边框，让编辑器安静下来，把任务精简成「今天要写什么」，并新增一套适合长时间写作的护眼主题。

- **更清爽的版面**　主页热力图、书封、待办和书架卡片去掉了外层硬框，改用底色和极淡的细线分区。各页面的顶栏高度、左右留白和书封比例统一，切换页面时不再晃动。
- **安静的编辑器顶栏**　顶栏与正文同一底色，平时不显示分隔线，往下滚动时才浮现；按钮平时变淡，鼠标移上去或用键盘选中时恢复清晰，把注意力留给正文。
- **AI 设置报错直接定位**　「生成参数」和「高级设置」平时收起。测试连接、获取模型列表或保存时，如果问题出在收起的字段（例如请求头、自定义路径、请求体 JSON），会自动展开并定位到出错的那一格。
- **任务精简**　任务行平时只显示勾选框和标题，点一下在原地展开，编辑日期、备注、关联作品、子任务和重复；侧栏按「今天 / 即将到来 / 以后 / 已完成」和各部作品整理，作品旁的小饼图显示待办进度；复制、移到今天、删除等操作收进「⋯」菜单。重复保留「每天」和「每周几」两种；旧任务里已有的复杂重复规则照常生效，描述、评论和附件在展开卡片里只读显示，导出时完整保留。
- **新主题「柔纸」**　米白纸张底配暖灰文字的低对比主题，适合长时间写作，所有文字仍满足无障碍对比度标准。可在「设置 → 外观」选择，原有的白色、黑色、米黄色主题不变。
- 修复：输入框光标的平滑动画在 Safari / WebKit 下偶尔会慢半拍。

## What's New in v0.28.0

This release is about a lighter interface: fewer nested frames, a quieter editor, tasks pared down to “what am I writing today”, and a new low-contrast theme for long writing sessions.

- **A cleaner layout**　The heatmap, covers, tasks and bookshelf cards on Home lose their outer frames and are separated by background tone and faint hairlines instead. Header height, side margins and cover proportions now match across pages, so switching pages no longer shifts things around.
- **A quiet editor header**　The header shares the page’s background and shows its divider only once you scroll. Its buttons stay faint until you point at them or reach them with the keyboard, leaving your attention on the text.
- **AI settings point to the problem**　“Generation” and “Advanced” stay collapsed. If a connection test, model list or save fails because of a collapsed field, such as a header, custom path or request body JSON, that section opens and the field is highlighted.
- **Simpler tasks**　A task row shows just a checkbox and title; click it to open it in place and edit the date, note, linked work, subtasks and repeat. The sidebar groups tasks into Today, Upcoming, Later and Completed, plus one entry per work with a small progress pie. Copy, move to today and delete live in the “⋯” menu. Repeats can be daily or on chosen weekdays. More complex repeat rules on existing tasks keep working, and existing descriptions, comments and attachments are shown read-only in the opened card and kept in exports.
- **New theme: Soft Paper**　Warm grey text on off-white paper, a low-contrast theme for long sessions that still meets accessibility contrast guidelines. Choose it in Settings → Appearance; the White, Dark and Beige themes are unchanged.
- Fixed: the smooth text caret in input fields could lag slightly in Safari / WebKit.
