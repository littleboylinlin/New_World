## v0.31.0 更新内容

这一版新增故事设定画布、带全书上下文的 AI 对话、设定提取与一致性检查，还能分别管理桌面备份的保留份数。

- **故事设定画布**　从编辑器顶栏的「设定」打开本书的白板。桌面版打开独立窗口，Web 版打开新标签页。画布铺满窗口，工具都是浮在上面的小岛：顶部一排工具（选择、套索、平移、卡片、箭头、文字、分组框、图片、橡皮擦，手机上在底部），左上角菜单收着卡片列表、标签、回收站、导出和设置，左下角是缩放和撤销。选中对象时才出现属性面板，面板和输入框会让开被操作的对象。布局与视角自动保存。
- **像白板一样顺手**　快捷键 V／1 选择、L 套索、H 平移、R／2 卡片、A／5 箭头、T／8 文字、F 分组框、9 图片、E／0 橡皮擦，画完自动回到选择，按 Q 或双击工具按钮可以锁定连续画。双击空白处新建卡片，双击卡片直接改名，回车打开完整编辑面板。支持框选、套索（按住拖出任意形状，松手自动闭合，选中被圈住的对象）、Shift 加选、拖动时的对齐参考线（按住 Ctrl／⌘ 暂时不吸附）、方向键微移、Ctrl／⌘ + D 复制一份、复制粘贴和撤销重做。滚轮或双指滑动平移，Ctrl／⌘ + 滚轮或捏合缩放，Shift + 1 适应全部内容；拖到画布边缘时自动滚动。
- **套索选择**　用套索可以圈出不规则的一片：卡片、文字、图片和分组框按中心点是否在圈内判定，箭头要起点、终点和中点都在圈内。画的时候显示虚线轨迹，松手后淡出，自动回到选择工具，可以直接拖动整组。手机上选择和套索共用一个按钮，再点一次切换，长按可直接选。
- **箭头连线**　用箭头工具从卡片里或边缘附近拖出，靠近另一张卡片、文字或连线时高亮，松手就建立关系；松在空白处则留下终点悬空的箭头，之后可再拖过去。箭头两端始终贴在卡片边缘，卡片移动或改变大小时跟着走。选中箭头后可拖两端改连、拖中点调成弧线，双击箭头在中间写关系名。同一对卡片同方向的关系不会重复建立，提示里可选「仍然新建」；反方向或多条关系自动错开成弧线。线宽和箭头在缩小时仍清楚可见。
- **自由卡片**　卡片只需填写名称，可添加别名、多个自定义彩色标签、任意键值字段和正文，人物、地点模板可以随意修改。画布上的卡片只显示名称和第一个标签的颜色，鼠标停在卡片上可在旁边看到别名、字段和正文，手机上选中卡片时在底部面板里显示。卡片列表可按名称或别名定位，删除的卡片可在回收站恢复。
- **关联章节**　卡片可以关联全书（在每一章都有效）、一个或多个章节，也可以不关联，仍可添加从某章起生效的状态。新卡默认关联编辑器正在写的那一章，编辑器换章节时跟着变；在设定窗口里手动改成全书、其他章节或多选几章后，就保持手动选的，也可以改回跟随编辑器。左上角只显示小说名，选中卡片时后面跟上卡片名；鼠标停在小说名上（手机上点一下）会浮出新卡默认关联，点开即可修改。编辑器「设定」按钮上的数字是本章有效的卡片数，全书卡片也算在内。
- **多个窗口**　左上角菜单的「新窗口」可以为同一本书再开一个设定窗口（Web 版是新标签页），各自保留自己的位置和缩放，内容实时同步。从编辑器点「设定」时会回到已经打开的窗口，不会每次都多开一个。
- **导出**　菜单里可以把整张画布或选中的内容导出为 PNG 图片，也可以直接导出本书的 `.newworld` 工程。
- **AI 对话**　从编辑器侧栏的「AI 对话」或「⋯」菜单打开。每部作品可以保留多段本机对话，支持继续聊、查看历史、确认删除、中途停止、复制回复。回复可插入正文的光标处，插入可以撤销，插入前的正文保留在历史版本里。
- **全书上下文**　AI 对话和浮动工具条的「自由提问」会带上当前章节、相关设定和已有的摘要。针对具体情节的问题可带上相关章的原文。设定卡只带当前章节及以前生效的状态。首次发送全书资料会提示确认，回复下方可以展开查看实际带入的资料。提问不会自动生成摘要，主动使用「总结整本」后就能复用结果。润色、改写等编辑功能仍只使用原有范围的文字。
- **选项式提问**　有几种合理理解且选错会造成较多返工时，AI 会给出可点选的答案。侧栏和选中提问都支持，也可以自己输入回答。
- **官网**　补齐「功能」「下载」「使用方法」的导航跳转，窄屏时三个链接可以换行显示。英文页脚显示「© 2026 Xiaonanhai」。
- **在线版**　底部提示为页面留出实际占用的空间，关闭后立即收回。修复写作热力图左侧月份标签截断，存储位置和说明文字可换行，导入、回收站和存储说明统一使用「原文件」。
- **从正文提取设定**　从设定窗口左上角菜单或空白画布进入。可提取本章、选中的章节或全书，按章显示进度并可停止。建议可以先勾选和修改。重名或别名相同的卡片默认合并，只补充别名和缺少的字段。新卡放在画布空位，整批采用可以一步撤销。
- **一致性检查**　从编辑器的 AI 菜单或设定窗口启动。检查用当章有效的设定核对选区、本章或全书，只报告明确的冲突。结果标出原句、卡片、字段和原因，可跳到原文或设定卡，预览差异后改正文，也可忽略并在本机记住。提取和检查默认使用当前编辑版本，也可选其他版本或原文件，结果注明所用版本。原文件只读，使用卡片的基础设定。两项功能都在主动启动后才发送请求。
- **备份保留份数**　桌面版可分别设置自动备份和恢复前备份保留 5、10、20 份或不限，默认各 10 份。缩减前会列出要清理的文件，确认后从最旧的开始删除。升级后已有的超额备份会在设置中提示，等确认后清理。手动备份由用户自行管理。
- **Windows 离线测试**　PDF 导出用实际保存结果判断完成，允许原生导出等待至 30 秒。重试使用独立的导入和续写记录。

### 兼容性

- 新版把本地书库升级到 IndexedDB v14，添加画布表、回收站的作品索引和本机 AI 对话表。v0.30.0 及更早版本无法直接打开升级后的书库。
- 全库备份使用格式 5，包含本机对话记录。单部 `.newworld` 工程使用 Schema 4，包含画布、布局、视角和卡片回收记录。v0.30.0 及更早版本会拒绝这两种新格式。新版可读取全库备份格式 1–5 和工程 Schema 1–4。
- 全库恢复会替换当前书库的画布和对话。格式 1–3 的备份恢复后画布为空，格式 1–4 的备份恢复后对话为空。需要回退时，请在升级前用旧版保留一份全库备份。
- v0.30.0 及更早版本也无法导入包含普通写作历史（`writing`）的工程。

## What's New in v0.31.0

This release adds a story canvas, AI Chat with book context, manuscript extraction and consistency checks, and separate retention limits for desktop backups.

- **Story canvas**　Open a work’s whiteboard from **Story settings** in the editor toolbar. The desktop app opens a separate window, and the web app opens a new tab. The canvas fills the window and every control floats on it: one row of tools at the top (Select, Lasso, Hand, Card, Arrow, Text, Frame, Image, Eraser, shown at the bottom on phones), a menu at the top left with the card list, tags, Trash, export and settings, and zoom and undo at the bottom left. Properties appear only for a selection, and panels and fields move aside from what you are editing. Layout and view are saved automatically.
- **Whiteboard feel**　Shortcuts: V/1 select, L lasso, H hand, R/2 card, A/5 arrow, T/8 text, F frame, 9 image, E/0 eraser. Drawing switches back to Select. Press Q or double-click a tool to keep it. Double-click the background for a new card, double-click a card to rename it, and press Enter for the full editor. Marquee, lasso and Shift selection, alignment guides while dragging (hold Ctrl/⌘ to turn snapping off), arrow-key nudging, Ctrl/⌘+D to duplicate, copy and paste, undo and redo. Scroll or swipe with two fingers to pan, Ctrl/⌘+scroll or pinch to zoom, Shift+1 to fit everything. The canvas scrolls when you drag to its edge.
- **Lasso selection**　Draw a free-form loop with the lasso; it closes when you let go. Cards, text, images and frames are picked when their middle is inside, arrows when their start, end and middle all are. The dashed trail fades after release, the Select tool comes back, and the group can be dragged right away. On phones Select and Lasso share one button: tap it again to switch, or hold it to choose.
- **Arrows**　With the Arrow tool, drag from inside or near a card. Cards, text and lines highlight as you approach. Releasing connects them. Releasing on the background leaves a loose end you can attach later. Arrow ends stay on card edges when cards move or resize. Select an arrow to drag its ends to other cards or curve it from the middle, and double-click it to name it in place. A second arrow in the same direction between two cards is caught, with **Add anyway** in the note. Opposite or extra arrows curve apart. Lines and arrowheads stay clear when zoomed out.
- **Free-form cards**　Cards require a name and can carry aliases, multiple custom colored tags, key/value fields and body text. Character and location templates are editable starting points. On the canvas a card shows its name and its first tag’s color; hover it to see aliases, fields and text beside it, or select it on a phone to see them in the bottom panel. Find cards by name or alias in the card list and restore deleted cards from Trash.
- **Chapter links**　A card can apply to the whole work (in every chapter), to one or more chapters, or to no chapter, and can still carry states that start at a chapter. New cards link to the chapter open in the editor and follow it as the editor moves. Choose the whole work, other chapters or several chapters in the story window and that choice stays, until you switch back to following the editor. The top left shows only the work’s name, followed by the selected card’s name. Hover the name (tap it on a phone) to see and change where new cards link. The number on the editor’s **Story settings** button counts the cards in effect in the current chapter, whole-work cards included.
- **Several windows**　**New window** in the top-left menu opens another story window on the same work (a new tab on the web). Each keeps its own position and zoom, and the content stays in sync. **Story settings** in the editor brings back the window already open instead of opening another each time.
- **Export**　From the menu, export the whole canvas or the selection as a PNG image, or export the work as a `.newworld` project.
- **AI Chat**　Open **AI Chat** from the editor sidebar or the **More** menu. Each work keeps its own conversations on this device. Resume a conversation, review or delete it, stop a reply as it streams, or copy the reply. Inserting a reply at the cursor is undoable and saves the previous text in chapter history.
- **Book context**　AI Chat and **Ask AI** in the selection toolbar can include the current chapter, related story cards and cached summaries. Questions about specific plot details can also include relevant chapter text. Card states are included through the current chapter. The first request for book context asks for consent, and the sources used are listed under each reply. Use **Summarize book** to populate the cache. Editing features such as polishing and rewriting continue to use the original text scope.
- **Questions with choices**　When several interpretations are plausible and a wrong guess would mean substantial rework, the AI can offer choices. Pick an answer or type your own in either AI Chat or **Ask AI**.
- **Website**　The Features, Download, and How to use links jump to their sections and wrap on narrow screens. The English footer shows “© 2026 Xiaonanhai”.
- **Web app**　Bottom notices reserve their own space and give it back when dismissed. Heatmap month labels at the left edge stay readable, storage and help text can wrap, and import, Trash, and storage copy consistently use “original file”.
- **Extract settings from the manuscript**　Start from the top-left story menu or the empty canvas. Choose the current chapter, selected chapters, or the whole book. Follow progress chapter by chapter and stop at any time. Select and edit proposals before applying. Matching names or aliases are merged by default, adding aliases and missing fields. New cards go in free spaces on the canvas, and one undo reverses the batch.
- **Consistency check**　Start from the editor’s AI menu or the story window. Check a selection, chapter, or whole book against the settings in effect for each chapter. Each clear contradiction shows the exact passage, card, field, and reason. Jump to the passage or card, review the differences before editing the manuscript, or ignore the finding and remember it on this device. Both tools default to the open editable version and also support other versions and original files. Results identify the source. Originals are read-only and use the card’s base settings. Both tools send requests when you start them.
- **Backup retention**　The desktop app keeps 5, 10, 20, or unlimited automatic and pre-restore backups, configured separately. Each defaults to 10. Lowering a limit shows the files eligible for removal and deletes the oldest after confirmation. Settings list any excess backups left after upgrading, with cleanup available on confirmation. Manual backups stay under your control.
- **Windows offline tests**　PDF export waits for the native save to finish, with up to 30 seconds for completion. Retries use separate import and continuation records.

### Compatibility

- The local library upgrades to IndexedDB v14, adding stores for the canvas and local AI conversations and a work index for Trash. New World v0.30.0 and earlier cannot open the upgraded library.
- Full-library backups use format 5 and include local conversations. Individual `.newworld` projects use Schema 4 and include the canvas, layout, view and trashed cards. New World v0.30.0 and earlier reject these exports. The new version reads backup formats 1–5 and project Schemas 1–4.
- A full-library restore replaces the current canvases and conversations. Backups in formats 1–3 restore with empty canvases, and formats 1–4 restore with no conversations. Keep a backup made in the older app before upgrading if you need to roll back.
- New World v0.30.0 and earlier also reject projects containing writing history (`writing`).
