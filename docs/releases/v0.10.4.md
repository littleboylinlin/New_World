## 首页写作统计

- 首页新增写作热力图，按周排列最近一年每天的颜色深浅；光标悬停或键盘聚焦某一天会显示当天写了多少字，今天单独标注。
- 热力图上方显示连续写作天数与总字数两张统计卡。字数沿用编辑器的非空白 Unicode 字符统计，英文界面同样以字符计。
- 每日字数在正文保存成功时按增量记录，只统计净增字数；导入、转换和复制不计入。写作历史属于全局记录，删除作品后仍保留连续天数与当日字数，总字数随当前书库变化。
- 支持方向键在格子间移动、手机宽度下内部横向滚动且不溢出页面，并遵循系统「减少动态效果」。界面文案提供中英文。
- IndexedDB 升级到 v4，新增 `writingDays` 存储；v3 老库打开时自动升级，原有正文与字数保留。

提供 Windows x64、Mac Apple Silicon 和 Intel 安装包。SHA-256 校验值见 `SHA256SUMS.txt`。

## Home writing stats

- The home page gains a writing heatmap: a year of days laid out by week, shaded by how much you wrote. Hovering or keyboard-focusing a day shows that day's character count, and today is marked separately.
- Two stat cards above the heatmap show your day streak and total characters. Counts reuse the editor's non-whitespace Unicode character counting, so the English interface also counts characters.
- Daily counts are recorded as a positive delta when a save commits; imports, conversions, and copies do not count. Writing history is global, so the streak and each day's count survive deleting a work, while the total reflects the current library.
- Arrow keys move between cells, phone widths scroll the heatmap internally without overflowing the page, and animations respect the system's "reduce motion". Interface copy is available in Chinese and English.
- IndexedDB is upgraded to v4 with a new `writingDays` store; v3 libraries upgrade automatically on open, keeping existing text and counts.

Includes Windows x64 and separate Mac Apple Silicon and Intel installers. Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
