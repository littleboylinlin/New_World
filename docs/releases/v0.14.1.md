## 弹窗和菜单

- 对话框、下拉菜单和外观面板换了新样式：边框和阴影更轻，对话框顶部有红黄蓝品牌色条，打开时背景轻微模糊。白色、黑色、米黄三个主题分别调整，黑色主题不再有刺眼的亮边。
- 焦点框改为蓝色光圈，输入框聚焦时边框变蓝，下拉框的箭头跟随主题颜色。
- 设置中选中的选项改为浅色底、红色描边和小对勾。菜单中的删除等操作用细线单独隔开。
- 从箭头按钮打开的菜单一律向下展开，下方空间不足时自动滚动页面；右键菜单仍在鼠标位置打开。

## 灵感面板

- 编辑器里的灵感从正文上方移到左侧章节目录下方，和目录一起固定在侧边，正文写到哪里都能看到，点「插入」放到光标处。
- 面板可以折叠，展开状态会被记住；窄屏下点工具栏的「灵感」会滚动到面板。

提供 Windows x64、Mac Apple Silicon 和 Intel 安装包。SHA-256 校验值见 `SHA256SUMS.txt`。

## Popups and menus

- Dialogs, drop-down menus and the appearance panel get a new look: lighter borders and shadows, the red, yellow and blue brand stripe on dialogs, and a softly blurred page behind them. The white, dark and beige themes are each tuned, and the dark theme no longer has glaring light edges.
- Focus rings are blue, text fields turn blue when focused, and drop-down arrows follow the theme.
- Selected options in Settings show a light fill, a red outline and a small tick. Delete and similar actions sit apart in menus, below a thin line.
- Menus opened from an arrow button always drop down, scrolling the page when there is little room below; right-click menus still open at the pointer.

## Notes panel

- The editor's notes move from above the text to under the chapter list, staying beside the text as it scrolls, so "Insert" puts a note at the cursor wherever you are.
- The panel folds and remembers whether it was open; on narrow screens the "Notes" toolbar button scrolls to it.

Includes Windows x64 and separate Mac Apple Silicon and Intel installers. Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
