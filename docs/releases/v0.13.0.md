## 首页写作统计

- 首页新增写作热力图，按周排列最近一年每天写了多少字，颜色越深写得越多；光标悬停或键盘聚焦某一天会显示当天字数，今天单独标注，方向键可在格子间移动。
- 热力图上方显示连续写作天数与总字数两张统计卡。计数沿用编辑器的文字处理软件式统计：每个汉字和中文标点算 1，英文等以空格分词的语言每个单词算 1，中英混排分别计算后相加。
- 每日字数在正文保存成功时按净增量记录，导入、转换和复制不计入。写作历史属于全局记录，删除作品后仍保留连续天数与当日字数，总字数随当前书库变化。
- 手机宽度下热力图内部横向滚动且不溢出页面，遵循系统「减少动态效果」，并适配浅色、深色与米色主题。界面文案提供中英文。
- IndexedDB 升级到 v4，新增全局 `writingDays` 存储；v3 老库打开时自动升级，原有正文与字数保留。

提供 Windows x64、Mac Apple Silicon 和 Intel 安装包。SHA-256 校验值见 `SHA256SUMS.txt`。

## Home writing stats

- The home page gains a writing heatmap: a year of days laid out by week, shaded darker the more you wrote. Hovering or keyboard-focusing a day shows that day's word count, today is marked separately, and arrow keys move between cells.
- Two stat cards above the heatmap show your day streak and total words. Counts reuse the editor's word-processor counting: each Chinese character or punctuation mark is one, each English (or other spaced-language) word is one, and mixed text adds both.
- Each day's count is recorded as a positive delta when a save commits; imports, conversions, and copies do not count. Writing history is global, so the streak and each day's count survive deleting a work, while the total reflects the current library.
- At phone widths the heatmap scrolls internally without overflowing the page, animations respect the system's "reduce motion", and it adapts to the light, dark, and beige themes. Interface copy is available in Chinese and English.
- IndexedDB is upgraded to v4 with a new global `writingDays` store; v3 libraries upgrade automatically on open, keeping existing text and counts.

Includes Windows x64 and separate Mac Apple Silicon and Intel installers. Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
